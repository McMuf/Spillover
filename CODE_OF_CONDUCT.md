# Code of Conduct

Spillover adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, accept criticism of your work gracefully,
and do not harass, insult or publish anyone's private information.

## Project-specific rules

- **Challenge claims, not people.** Disagree with evidence, in a challenge, a counter-claim or a
  comment.
- No claims or comments that target individuals, promote specific trades, or pump thinly traded
  assets. Spillover is research, not a signal service.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub: use **Report content** on the comment
or discussion, or open a private security advisory from the repository's Security tab if the issue
is sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and
temporarily or permanently ban accounts that break this code, following the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
